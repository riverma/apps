How to install

These are web apps. No store, no download. Open an app, then add it to your home screen or dock. It then launches full-screen like a native app.

iPhone / iPad, in Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android, in Chrome

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm. The app appears in your app drawer.

Desktop, in Firefox, Chrome, Edge, and others

  1. Every app runs in any desktop browser.
  2. To pin it as a standalone app, use Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar (a monitor with a down-arrow), or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install.

Most apps work fully offline once installed. The map-based apps need an internet connection for maps and routing.
